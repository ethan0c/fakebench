See how your score compares

After each test, you get a score and an estimated percentile to show where you stand among other results. You can also look back at the images you missed, with the model name for AI images and the source for real photos.

How are people doing?

FakeBench tracks how often players get the answer right over the past seven days. Each completed test contributes to that average. It reflects results on this site, so it can change as different people play and new images are added.
